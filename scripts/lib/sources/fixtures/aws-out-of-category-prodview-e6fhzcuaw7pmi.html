      The core functionality of EVA is designed to address the challenges associated with identity verification in IVR systems and agent conversations. It streamlines and secures the verification process, reducing the time-consuming and often irritating steps required from customers. EVA offers both active and passive verification methods to cater to different preferences and use cases.

      In the IVR, EVA allows callers to access personalized self-service options simply by speaking to the system. This removes the need for customers to remember and input cumbersome authentication information, enhancing their experience while maintaining security.

      For agent-assisted interactions, EVA provides a pre-verified call, ensuring that agents are dealing with legitimate customers from the start. Throughout the conversation, EVA continuously and passively verifies identity of the caller, further enhancing security and fraud prevention.

      Built on a secure, certified AWS SaaS foundation, EVA delivers seamless, and native integration with Amazon Connect. Powered by Auraya's ArmorVox engine, EVA follows its secure design principles ensuring that it has been built to be biometrically secure while complying with the various regulatory compliance globally.

      We support AWS Private Offers to facilitate custom requirements. If you require larger volume pricing tiers, please reach out to Auraya Sales via info@aurayasystems.com .

      • Built on a secure, certified AWS SaaS foundation, EVA delivers seamless and native integration with Amazon Connect to ensure enterprise-grade protection.       Dimensions summary

      You buy this voice authentication service on a 12-month contract. Pick one of four subscription tiers based on your expected monthly volume. Each tier includes a set block of usage units credited at the start of every billing period: 80,000, 100,000, 250,000, or 500,000 units. If you use more than your included amount, overage charges apply. Overage rates step through usage bands: up to 80,000 units, 80,001 to 100,000, 100,001 to 250,000, 250,001 to 500,000, and a volume-discounted rate for all usage beyond 500,000 units. Higher usage bands are metered at their applicable tier rate.

      Top-of-mind questions for buyers

      What counts as one usage unit for billing purposes?
      A unit maps to a voice biometric transaction, such as verifying a caller's identity by comparing their speech to a stored voiceprint. Verification happens in the IVR or during agent conversations. Each authentication event consumes units, so higher call and verification volumes increase your monthly usage.
      What happens if I use more units than my tier includes?
      Overage charges apply only to usage beyond your included block. Rates step through usage bands: up to 80,000, 80,001 to 100,000, 100,001 to 250,000, 250,001 to 500,000, and a volume-discounted rate beyond 500,000. Each band is metered at its applicable tier rate, so extra usage is charged separately.
      How do included units and overage charges combine on my invoice?
      Your subscription tier sets a fixed monthly block of included units, credited at the start of each billing period. Usage within that block is covered by the subscription fee. Usage above it triggers overage charges at the applicable band rate. Both the subscription and any overages appear on the same bill.
